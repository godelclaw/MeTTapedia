# Weighted matchings and the historical finite search

The canonical mathematics is maintained in subject-based library families:

- [Matching theory](../mettapedia/Mettapedia/Combinatorics/Matching)
- [GHZ weighted-matching equations](../mettapedia/Mettapedia/Combinatorics/Matching/GHZ)
- [Polynomial certificate semantics](../mettapedia/Mettapedia/Algebra/Polynomial)

These contain the reusable structural results, normalization, algebraic
certificate soundness and the bridges to the Krenn–Gu statement. The general
complex conjecture remains open; conditional results keep their hypotheses
explicit.

The historical Lean 4.31 finite-search sources are preserved on
[`krenn/search`](https://github.com/godelclaw/MeTTapedia/tree/036ead480fc86ddd2c73bcfc8b7f314e5e93f18a/lean/krenn).
That pinned archive documents its focused verification. The complete generated
corpus was not rebuilt during relocation, and its partial six-site search is
not a proof of the general conjecture.
