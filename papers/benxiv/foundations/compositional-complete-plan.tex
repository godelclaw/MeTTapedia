\section{Current proof plan: a completed classical reconstruction}
\label{sec:complete-compositional-plan}

\textbf{Status: 28 September 2026.}
The complete assembly is kernel-checked in commit \texttt{b99eaf006}.
Its central theorem for graphs is
\path{FourColor.CompositionalComplete.colorable_of_planeDrawing}:
every finite simple graph supplied with a noncrossing path drawing in the
real plane admits a proper four-colouring. Empty and disconnected graphs
and isolated vertices are included. The drawing contains geometric data,
not a colouring assumption.

The same assembly proves
\path{FourColor.CompositionalComplete.supported_source_construction}
and \path{FourColor.CompositionalComplete.supported_source_sweep}.
Every bridgeless spherical cubic source map has an exact construction trace
with compatible colouring witnesses, and every complete valid source vertex
sweep admits such choices. The construction preserves the source's actual
vertices, edges and cyclic order, including parallel edges.

\paragraph{Relation to the compositional note.}
The note's three levels remain the organizing principle. At Level~0,
minimality supplies a colouring of a smaller contracted map; reconstruction
then colours the original. At Level~1, literal graph constructors and exact
source coverage give operational histories. At Level~2, a colouring of a
composite is equivalent to a compatible pair of colourings of its pieces:
\[
 W(P;Q;x,z)\ \simeq\ \coprod_y W(P;x,y)\times W(Q;y,z).
\]
Nonemptiness yields relational composition, and finite cardinality yields
the boundary-matrix multiplication law. These statements retain the actual
witnesses, so they support replacement inside a surrounding context.

\paragraph{The repaired global argument.}
The changed premise is coverage. The complete proof obtains it from the
classical unavoidable configurations and discharging argument, using the
mathematics of Robertson, Sanders, Seymour and Thomas through the
Gonthier--Werner formalization and Chris Emery's Lean port. It does not derive
universal corridor supply or a globally finite image of exact Count from
bounded defect. Its implication chain is
\[
\begin{gathered}
\text{checked reducibility + a fitted configuration + minimality}\\
\Longrightarrow\text{compatible disk and exterior witnesses}\\
\Longrightarrow\text{an actual colouring of the alleged obstruction},
\end{gathered}
\]
while discharging forces such a fitted configuration in every minimal
counterexample. The two arguments exclude minimal counterexamples and give
the graph and source-construction endpoints above.

In \path{EmbeddedReconstruction}, the select-and-glue GSLT route has an
explicit witness and its output is the reconstructed colouring consumed by
\path{ClassicalAssembly.no_embedded_reduction}. Selection uses classical
choice. No executable Kempe-move sequence is claimed, and no theorem requires
every partial colouring to survive every next operation.

\paragraph{What the complete proof consumes.}
All 633 configuration certificates remain. Semantic rotation, reflection
and paired-charge transport replace 231 independent discharging checks at
arities nine through eleven: 11,363 become 11,132, with 218 finite input
identities or classifications counted separately. All 32 discharging-rule
labels remain. No configuration-family reducibility saving, smaller
unavoidable catalogue, runtime improvement or artifact-size saving is claimed.
\path{MettapediaFourColor.CompleteAudit} follows the graph, construction and
sweep endpoints, checks their geometric and shared-proof dependencies, and
rejects the original unconditional theorem and 236 superseded checker
declarations. The audited axiom set is
\(\{\texttt{propext},\texttt{Classical.choice},\texttt{Quot.sound}\}\).

\paragraph{Where the original programme continues.}
The tube, facial-sweep, Kempe-recomposition and support theorems are available
as uniform candidates for further compression. Literal zigzag tubes now have
the sufficient support-stabilization bound \(2k(k-1)\) at circumference
\(k\geq3\), hence 40 rings at circumference five. This does not prove that
every target contains such a tube. The current boundary-triangle subdivision
theorem decomposes all four-colourings into an old-colouring branch and a
contracted-colouring branch with two fresh-colour choices. To remove a family
of configuration checks, its catalogue realizations and the reducibility
transport through both branches must still be proved and consumed by the
complete assembly.

The independent corridor/finite-base proposal remains recorded in
Section~\ref{sec:compositional-proof-spine}; proving the classical repair
does not establish its distinctive premises. The concise
\href{fourcolor-compositional/README.md}{proof-plan guide} maps the original
levels and playbook phases to the current Lean modules, records the reusable
graph-representation and MeTTa results, and links the measured compression
ledger. It is the entry point for the current development.
